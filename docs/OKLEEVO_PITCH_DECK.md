##  Slide 2: The Problem (SaaS Sprawl & Cross-Border Friction)

Modern SMEs lose **30% of their operational efficiency and \$500–\$1,200/month** juggling disconnected point solutions:

1. **Tool Fatigue & Context Switching:**
   * Zoom for video calls, Calendly for booking, Monday.com for tasks, QuickBooks for accounting, Mailchimp for email, and DocuSign for e-signatures.
2. **Exorbitant Stack Costs:**
   * Point solutions charge \$15–\$40/seat per tool, making a modern tech stack inaccessible for small businesses and agencies.
3. **The Global & Emerging Market Payment Divide:**
   * Standard SaaS platforms only accept Western credit cards via Stripe. In emerging markets (e.g. Africa's 44M+ SMEs), cross-border card decline rates exceed **70%**, shutting out buyers who rely on **Mobile Money (M-Pesa, MoMo)** and **Local Bank Transfers**.

## 4. Product Pillars & Capabilities

### 1. Virtual HQ & Realtime Collaboration
* **In-Browser Video/Audio Rooms:** Built on LiveKit WebRTC with screen sharing, participant controls, and meeting duration timers with auto-expiry.
* **Presence & Activity Feeds:** Real-time visibility into who is active, in calls, or collaborating.

### 2. Layer 2 Client Engagement (Zero-Login Edge)
* **Branded Booking Pages:** Custom public pages (`/book/[businessId]/[slug]`) with custom availability rules.
* **Zero-Login Secure Guest Rooms:** Clients join video consultations with a 6-digit email PIN (`/room/[id]`) without needing an account.
* **Isolated File Sandbox:** Guest file sharing backed by an isolated, malware-scanned MinIO S3 bucket (`okleevo-client-sandbox`).

### 3. Core Business Operations (ERP / CRM / Invoicing)
* **CRM & Sales Pipeline:** Lead tracking, contact history, and deal progression.
* **Multi-Currency Invoicing:** Instant payment links, automated tax calculation, and ledger reconciliation.
* **Agile Project Management:** Kanban boards, task assignments, milestones, and project margin tracking.
* **Email Campaigns & Ticketing:** Built-in email broadcast engine (Postal) and customer helpdesk portal.

### 4. Built-in Multimodal AI Intelligence Studio
* **Groq Llama 3.3 (70B):** Ultra-fast marketing copy, campaign drafting, and client communication generation.
* **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash:** Multimodal document processing and automated receipt / UK HMRC VAT extraction.
