AI Notes (shown in-app as "Notes Studio") is a note-taking tool for meetings, brainstorms, research, and personal notes — with an optional one-click AI pass that adds a short summary and a list of action items to a note.

## What it's for

- Writing and organising notes by type: Meeting, Brainstorm, Document, Tasks, Research, or Personal
- Tagging notes and searching across title, content, and tags
- Pinning important notes to the top and starring favourites
- Generating an AI summary and action-item list for a note as you create it

## Views

Switch between **Grid** and **List** view, and filter by type using the pills above the note list. Pinned notes always appear in their own section at the top.

## Stats at a glance

The header shows total Notes, Starred count, notes created this Week, and total Action items across all your notes.

## Who it's for

Anyone who takes notes regularly and wants a fast way to pull out a summary and next steps without re-reading everything.
