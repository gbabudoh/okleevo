## Tutorial: Capturing a meeting with AI-generated action items

1. Click **New Note** and select the **Meeting** type.
2. Title it with the meeting name and date, and paste or type your raw notes into Content.
3. Turn on **AI Assist** and leave the default provider selected.
4. Click **Create Note** — Okleevo generates a short summary and a list of action items alongside your notes.
5. Open the note afterward to check off action items as you complete them.

## Tutorial: Keeping a running research log

1. Create a note with the **Research** type for each topic you're investigating.
2. Add relevant tags (e.g. "competitor", "pricing") so you can find related notes later via search.
3. Star the notes that turn out to be most useful, and pin the one you refer to most often.

## Tutorial: Finding a note again later

1. Use the search box with a keyword from the title, content, or a tag.
2. If you remember the type but not the exact wording, filter by type first (e.g. Brainstorm) and then scan the list.

## Tutorial: Saving a note for use outside Okleevo

1. Open the note.
2. Click **Save** to download it as a `.txt` file, or **Copy** to paste it directly into another document or email.
