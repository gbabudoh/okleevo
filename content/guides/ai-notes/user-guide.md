title: AI Notes User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for creating and managing notes.
---

## Creating a note

1. Click **New Note**.
2. Pick a type (Meeting, Brainstorm, Document, Tasks, Research, or Personal) — Meeting is selected by default.
3. Enter a Title (required) and Content.
4. Optionally add comma-separated Tags.
5. Choose an AI provider (Groq or Gemini) and toggle **AI Assist** to On if you want an automatic summary and action items.
6. Click **Create Note**.

If AI Assist is on, the button shows "Generating…" while the summary is produced. If the AI call fails for any reason, you'll see a warning that AI Assist failed — the note still saves, just without a summary or action items.

## Viewing a note

Click any note card to open it. You'll see the type, date, tags, the AI Summary (if generated), the full note content, and any Action Items as a checklist.

## Starring and pinning

- Click the star icon (on a card or inside the note) to mark a note as a favourite — starred notes count toward the "Starred" stat.
- Pinning is done from inside the note view (click the pin icon) — pinned notes are grouped in their own section above the rest of your notes.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search box to match a note's title, content, or tags, and the type pills to narrow the list to one category.

## Copying, downloading, and deleting

From inside a note, use **Copy** to copy the title and content to your clipboard, **Save** to download it as a `.txt` file, or **Delete** to remove it permanently.
