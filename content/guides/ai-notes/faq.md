title: AI Notes FAQ
summary: Common questions and troubleshooting for AI Notes.
---

## What happens if AI Assist fails?

Your note still saves — you'll just see a warning that AI Assist failed, and the note won't have a summary or action items. You can always add your own notes on what needs to happen next.

## Does switching the AI provider change which exact model is used?

It chooses which provider (Groq or Gemini) handles your request — think of it as picking a provider rather than a specific model version.

## Can I add participants or a meeting duration to a note?

Those fields exist on a note's record and will display if present, but there's currently no field in the note-creation form to set them.

## Why is "Week" not resetting on a fixed day like Monday?

The Week stat is a rolling count of notes created in the last 7 days, not a fixed calendar week — it updates continuously rather than resetting at a specific point.

## Can I pin a note directly from the note list?

Not from the card — open the note first, then use the pin icon inside it.

## Do the microphone and video icons in the New Note form record anything?

No, those are placeholders for a future feature and don't currently do anything when clicked.
