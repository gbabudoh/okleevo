summary: Step-by-step instructions for building a page from a template.
---

## Creating a page

1. Click **Create New** (or **Create First Page** if you don't have any yet).
2. Pick a category tab — All, Marketing, Events, Sales, Personal — and click a template card.
3. Your new page is created as a draft immediately, and the editor opens automatically so you can customise it.

## Editing a page

Open a page (or it opens automatically right after creation) to edit:

- **Core Configuration** — Page Title, Slug, and Status (Published, Draft, or Archived).
- **SEO Optimization** — Meta Title and Meta Description.
- **Page Sections** — the list of sections that came with your chosen template (e.g. Hero, Features, CTA, Footer).

Click **Save & Deploy** to keep your changes, or **Discard** to close without saving.

## Browsing your pages

Use the search box, the Status filter (Published/Draft/Archived), and the Grid/List toggle to find a page.

## Page actions

Each page's "⋮" menu offers **Copy URL**, **Analytics** (views/conversions for that page), **Settings**, and **Delete**.

## Deleting a page

Choose **Delete** from the "⋮" menu, then confirm. This is permanent.
