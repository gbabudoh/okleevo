---
title: Micro Pages FAQ
summary: Common questions about the Micro Pages preview.
---

## Is my page actually live on the internet once I save it?

Not yet — this module is an early preview, so "Save & Deploy" keeps your changes in the current session but doesn't publish the page anywhere. Pages are also lost when you refresh, since nothing is saved to your account yet.

## Do the view and conversion stats reflect real visitors?

Not currently — every new page starts at 0 views/conversions and there's no real traffic tracking behind them yet.

## Can I reorder or add sections to a page?

The Page Sections list shows what came with your template, but reordering and adding new sections aren't functional yet.

## Why didn't "Copy URL" put anything on my clipboard?

That action currently shows you the link in a dialog rather than copying it automatically — you can select and copy it manually from there.

## Will my pages be saved once this feature is fully released?

Not from anything created in the current preview — check back as the module develops for persistent, published pages.
