The Cashflow module gives you a real-time view of money moving in and out of your business, drawn from your actual invoices and expenses.

## What it's for

- Tracking income vs. expenses over time (week, month, quarter, year)
- Quickly logging a one-off income or expense entry
- Seeing key health metrics: Cash Runway, Savings Rate, and Burn Rate
- Reviewing a breakdown of spending by category
- Exporting your cashflow report

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants a running pulse on the business's financial health without digging through separate invoicing and expense records.
