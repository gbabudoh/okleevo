## Where does the data on this page come from?

Your real invoices and expenses — Cashflow doesn't use separate mock data, it's a live view built from the same records you manage in Invoicing and Expenses.

## What's the difference between adding an entry here vs. in Invoicing or Expenses?

Nothing structurally — a quick "Income" entry here creates a paid invoice, and a quick "Expense" entry creates an expense record, exactly as if you'd added them from those modules directly. Cashflow is just a faster way to log a one-off entry without leaving the page.

## Why is my Cash Runway showing "N/A"?

That happens when there isn't enough expense history yet to calculate a meaningful average monthly burn rate.

## Can I edit an entry I added from Cashflow later?

Yes — edit or delete it directly from the **Recent Transactions** list, or find the same record in Invoicing/Expenses.
