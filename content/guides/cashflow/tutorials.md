## Tutorial: Logging a cash sale that isn't a formal invoice

1. Click **New Entry** and choose **Income**.
2. Describe what it was for, enter the amount and date, and pick a category (e.g. Sales).
3. Save — this creates a paid record automatically, so it's reflected in your income figures without you needing to raise a full invoice.

## Tutorial: Understanding a sudden drop in Cash Runway

1. Check your **Burn Rate** — has average monthly spending gone up?
2. Switch the time range to **This Quarter** or **This Year** to see whether it's a one-off spike or a genuine trend.
3. Review the expense category breakdown to see where the increase is coming from.

## Tutorial: Monthly cashflow review

1. Set the time range to **This Month**.
2. Compare income vs. expenses on the chart.
3. Export the data if you want to keep a record or share it with your accountant.
