## Adding a quick entry

1. Click **New Entry**.
2. Choose **Income** or **Expense**.
3. Fill in the description, amount, date, and category.
4. Save. Income entries are recorded as paid invoices; expense entries go straight into your Expenses records — so they show up consistently across the whole app, not just here.

## Editing or deleting an entry

Use the edit and delete icons on any row in the **Recent Transactions** list. If you change an entry's type (income ↔ expense), Okleevo automatically moves the underlying record to the right place.

## Changing your time range

Use the dropdown in the header to switch between This Week, This Month, This Quarter, and This Year — the chart and stats update to match.

## Reading your health metrics

- **Cash Runway** — a rough estimate of how many months your current reserves would last at your average spending rate.
- **Savings Rate** — the percentage of income you're keeping after expenses.
- **Burn Rate** — your average monthly spend.

## Exporting

Use the export icon in the header to download your monthly cashflow data as a CSV.
