## Can I call someone who's offline?

The Video and Voice buttons work regardless of their online status, but they're far more likely to notice and join if they're currently online — check the indicator on their card first.

## Does the chat update in real time?

It checks for new messages automatically every few seconds while the chat drawer is open, so you don't need to close and reopen it to see a reply.

## Is my chat history saved?

Yes — reopening the chat drawer with the same teammate shows your previous conversation, not just new messages.

## Who can see my team calls and chats?

Everything here is scoped to your own organisation — it's described as encrypted and never shared externally.

## What's the difference between a one-on-one call and the General Meeting?

A one-on-one call (Video/Voice on a teammate's card) rings that specific person. **General Meeting** opens a single shared room that any teammate can join at any time, useful for an open, whole-team huddle.

## Can I share my screen on a call?

Video calls support screen sharing as part of the meeting room.
