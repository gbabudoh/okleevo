---
title: Collaboration User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for calls and chat.
---

## Seeing who's online

Your team is listed as a grid of cards, each showing their name, role, and an online/offline indicator. The header shows a running count — e.g. "3 / 8 online".

## Starting a one-on-one call

On any teammate's card, click **Video** for a video call or **Voice** for an audio-only call. This opens the meeting room immediately and notifies that teammate.

## Starting a call with the whole team

Click **General Meeting** in the header to open a shared video room anyone on the team can join.

## Leaving a call

While in a meeting, use the on-screen controls to leave — this returns you to the team grid.

## Messaging a teammate

1. Click **Chat** on their card to open the chat drawer.
2. Type your message and press send (or hit Enter).
3. The conversation refreshes automatically every few seconds, so replies from your teammate appear without needing to reopen the drawer.

## Closing the chat drawer

Click the **X** in the drawer header, or click outside the drawer, to close it.
