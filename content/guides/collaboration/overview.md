Collaboration is Okleevo's built-in team hub — see who's online, start a video or voice call, or message a teammate directly, without leaving Okleevo for a separate Slack or Zoom subscription and login.

## What it's for

- Seeing your whole team at a glance, with a live online/offline indicator for each person
- Starting a one-on-one video or voice call with any teammate
- Starting a shared **General Meeting** that anyone on the team can join
- Sending direct chat messages to a teammate, with message history

## Privacy

All communication here is described as end-to-end encrypted and locked to your own organisation — it's never shared externally.

## Who it's for

Any team that wants a quick way to check who's around and jump into a call or conversation without scheduling anything in advance.
