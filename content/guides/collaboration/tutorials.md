## Tutorial: Jumping on a quick call with a colleague

1. Find their card in the team grid and check they're marked Online.
2. Click **Video** for a face-to-face call, or **Voice** if you just need to talk.
3. When you're done, use the on-screen leave control to end the call and return to the team grid.

## Tutorial: Running a whole-team huddle

1. Click **General Meeting** in the header.
2. Share that you've started it (e.g. via chat) so teammates know to join — anyone can enter the same shared room.

## Tutorial: Having a quick written conversation

1. Click **Chat** on a teammate's card.
2. Send your message — it appears instantly on your side, and the drawer keeps checking for their reply automatically.
3. Scroll up in the drawer to review earlier messages in your conversation history.

## Tutorial: Checking in on team availability

1. Glance at the online count in the header ("N / total online") for a quick read on who's around right now.
2. Use each card's online/offline dot for a more precise, per-person check before starting a call.
