## Setting up your Chart of Accounts

Before you can record journal entries, you need active accounts. If your Chart of Accounts is empty, Okleevo will prompt you to initialize the default set (covering Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, and Expenses) with one click — from there you can add, edit, or deactivate individual accounts on the **Chart of Accounts** tab.

## Recording a journal entry

1. Click **New Entry**.
2. Enter a description and reference for the transaction.
3. Add debit and credit lines against the relevant accounts — the entry must balance (total debits = total credits) before it can be saved.
4. Save the entry. It will appear in your **Journal**.

## Reviewing your books

- **Journal** — search and filter every entry you've recorded, by description, reference, or status.
- **Trial Balance** — confirms your total debits equal your total credits across all accounts.
- **Reports** — generates financial statements from your ledger.
- **Year-End** — walks you through closing out the accounting period.

## Exporting

Use the **Export** button in the header to download your accounting records.
