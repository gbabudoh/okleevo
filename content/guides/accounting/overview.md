The Accounting module gives you full double-entry bookkeeping — a proper Chart of Accounts, journal entries, trial balance, financial reports, and year-end close, all built for UK SMEs.

## What it's for

- Maintaining a Chart of Accounts (Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Expenses)
- Recording journal entries with balanced debits and credits
- Reviewing your Trial Balance to make sure your books are in balance
- Generating financial reports
- Running your Year-End close process

## Who it's for

Business owners who want proper double-entry books (not just a spreadsheet), and bookkeepers or accountants who need a structured ledger to work from.
