## Tutorial: Setting up your books for the first time

1. Go to the **Chart of Accounts** tab and initialize the default accounts if you haven't already.
2. Review the default accounts and adjust names or add new ones to match how your business actually operates.
3. Start recording journal entries for your opening balances and ongoing transactions.

## Tutorial: Recording a transaction correctly

1. Identify which accounts are affected — for example, a cash sale affects both a Revenue account (credit) and your Cash/Bank account (debit).
2. Create a new journal entry with matching debit and credit lines for the same total amount.
3. Save it, then check the **Trial Balance** to confirm everything still balances.

## Tutorial: Closing out a period

1. Review your **Trial Balance** to catch any entries that don't balance before you close.
2. Generate your reports for the period from the **Reports** tab.
3. Use the **Year-End** tab to walk through closing the accounting period.
