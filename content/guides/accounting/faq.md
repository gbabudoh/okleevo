## Why can't I save my journal entry?

Double-entry bookkeeping requires debits to equal credits on every entry. If the save is blocked, check that your total debit and credit lines match exactly.

## What happens if I haven't set up my Chart of Accounts yet?

You'll see a prompt to initialize the default Chart of Accounts (Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Expenses) with one click before you can record your first journal entry.

## What's the difference between the Journal and the Trial Balance?

The **Journal** is the full list of individual entries you've recorded. The **Trial Balance** is a summary check that totals every account's debits and credits to confirm your books are internally consistent.

## Does this replace my accountant?

No — Accounting gives you a proper, structured ledger, but your accountant still reviews and finalises your official financial statements and filings.
