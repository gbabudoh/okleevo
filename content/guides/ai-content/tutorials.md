## Tutorial: Writing a blog post draft

1. Choose the **Blog Post** template (Content category).
2. Enter your Topic, pick a Tone (Professional, Casual, Friendly, Authoritative, or Conversational), choose a Length (300–500 / 500–1000 / 1000–2000 words), and add a few Keywords.
3. Click **Generate Content** and review the draft in the Output panel.
4. Click **Save** to download it as a `.txt` file you can paste into your CMS or editor.

## Tutorial: Drafting a social campaign across platforms

1. Choose **Social Post**, set the Platform (Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, or TikTok), describe your Topic/Message, pick a Style, and choose whether to include hashtags.
2. Generate, copy the result, and repeat with a different Platform selected to get a version tailored to each channel.

## Tutorial: Turning a feature list into ad copy

1. Choose **Ad Copy**, select the Ad Platform (Google, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter Ads), describe your Product/Service and its Main Benefit, and add a Call-to-Action.
2. Generate a few variations by tweaking the Main Benefit wording and comparing results — each generation is independent, so you can quickly try a few angles.

## Tutorial: Building a press release

1. Choose **Press Release**, fill in the Announcement, Company Name, Key Details, and a Quote Source.
2. Generate, then copy the text into your usual release template or distribution tool.
