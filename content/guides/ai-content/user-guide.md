## Choosing a template

1. Open **AI Content**. You'll see a grid of 12 templates.
2. Optionally filter by category using the pills above the grid: All, Content, Social, Email, Ads, Video, E-commerce, SEO, PR, Branding.
3. Click a template card to open its generator.

## Filling in the form

Each template asks for a different set of fields — for example, a Blog Post asks for Topic, Tone, Length, and Keywords, while a Social Post asks for Platform, Topic/Message, Style, and whether to include hashtags. Fields are a mix of text boxes, text areas, and dropdown selects.

You don't need to fill in every field — the **Generate Content** button becomes active as soon as at least one field has something in it, though more detail generally produces a better result.

## Generating content

Click **Generate Content**. While the AI is working, the Output panel shows a loading state; once done, your generated text appears there along with three quick stats: word count, estimated read time, and character count.

## Using the result

- Click **Copy** to copy the text to your clipboard.
- Click **Save** to download it as a `.txt` file.
- Click **Back** to return to the template grid and start a new piece of content — note that leaving the generator clears the current result, so copy or save first if you want to keep it.

## Switching templates

Going back and choosing a different template resets the form and output — each template's session is independent.
