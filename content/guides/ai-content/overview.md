AI Content (shown in-app as "Content Studio") turns a short form into ready-to-use written copy — blog posts, social captions, ad copy, product descriptions, and more — using an AI model.

## What it's for

- Picking from 12 content templates covering Content, Social, Email, Ads, Video, E-commerce, SEO, and Branding
- Filling in a few fields specific to the template (topic, tone, platform, etc.)
- Generating a ready-to-use draft in seconds, then copying or downloading it

## Templates available

Blog Post, Social Post, Email Campaign, Product Description, Ad Copy, Video Script, Landing Page, Press Release, Case Study, SEO Article, LinkedIn Post, and Slogan.

## How generation works

Content is generated by an AI model (Groq by default, automatically falling back to Gemini if Groq isn't available) based on the fields you fill in.

## Who it's for

Anyone who needs a fast first draft of marketing or business copy without starting from a blank page.

## Good to know

Generated content isn't saved anywhere in your account — it only exists in the Output panel for your current visit. Use **Copy** or **Save** (download) before you navigate away if you want to keep it.
