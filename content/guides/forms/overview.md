---
title: Forms Overview
summary: Build and publish custom forms, then track responses.
---

Forms lets you build a custom data-collection form, publish it as a public page, and track how many responses it collects.

## What it's for

- Building a form field-by-field: label, type (Short Text, Long Text, Email, Phone, Number, Date, Dropdown, Checkbox), and whether it's required
- Publishing a form as a standalone public page anyone with the link can fill in
- Tracking total responses and average responses per form
- Optionally forwarding every submission to a webhook URL

## Categories

Forms can be tagged Contact, Survey, HR, Event, Sales, Support, Accounting, Contract, or Other.

## Statuses

A form is **Active** (public and accepting responses), **Draft** (private, not reachable by its public link), or **Closed**.

## Who it's for

Anyone who needs to collect structured information from clients, leads, or team members without building a page from scratch.
