---
title: Forms User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for building and publishing a form.
---

## Creating a form

1. Click **Create Form**.
2. Enter a Form name (required), choose a Category, choose a Status, and optionally add a Description.
3. Add fields one at a time: type a Field label, choose a Type, toggle Required if needed, and click **Add**. Repeat for every field you need.
4. Click **Create Form** — this stays disabled until you've entered a name and added at least one field.

## Publishing your form

If your form was created as a Draft, open its **Details** view and click **Activate** to make it public. Once active, use **Copy Link** (on the card or inside Details) to grab its public URL and share it — only Active forms are reachable at their public link.

## Viewing responses and details

Open **Details** on any form to see its response count, field count, current status, and the full list of fields with their type and required status.

## Editing a form

Click **Edit** to change the name, description, category, status (including Closed), or add a Webhook URL so every new submission is also posted to another system. The field list itself isn't editable from this modal.

## Exporting

The **Export Data** button inside Details downloads a small CSV summary (form name, total responses, field count, export date) — it's a summary, not a row-by-row dump of every response.

## Deleting a form

Click **Delete**, then confirm. This permanently removes the form and every response it has collected.
