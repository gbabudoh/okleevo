## Tutorial: Working a new lead from first contact to close

1. Add the lead as a new contact, starting at the **New** pipeline stage.
2. Once you've reached out, move them to **Contacted**.
3. After sending pricing or a proposal, move them to **Proposal**, then **Negotiation** as the conversation develops.
4. Close the deal by moving them to **Closed Won** — or **Closed Lost** if it doesn't go ahead, so your pipeline stays accurate.
5. Log the outcome as a note on their record for future reference.

## Tutorial: Reviewing your pipeline health

1. Switch to list view for a quick scan of every contact and their current stage.
2. Look for contacts stuck in **Contacted** or **Proposal** for a long time — these are good candidates for a follow-up.

## Tutorial: Keeping a paper trail on a client

1. Every email you send from a contact's record is automatically logged under their **Communication** tab.
2. Use the **Notes** tab to record anything that happened outside of email — a phone call, a meeting, or an internal decision about the account.
