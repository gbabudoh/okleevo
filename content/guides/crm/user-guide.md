## Adding a contact

1. Click the **+** / **Add Contact** button.
2. Fill in the contact's name, email, phone, company, and type (business or individual).
3. Choose a starting pipeline stage — new contacts usually start at **New**.
4. Save the contact.

## Viewing and switching between grid and list

Use the view toggle above the contact list to switch between a card **grid** view and a compact **list** (table) view — whichever suits how you like to scan your contacts.

## Opening a contact record

Click any contact card or row to open their full record, which is organised into three tabs:

- **Info** — contact details and pipeline stage
- **Communication** — email history, filterable by Sent/Received
- **Notes** — internal notes only your team can see

## Moving a contact through the pipeline

Open the contact, go to the **Info** tab, and update their pipeline stage as the relationship progresses — from **New** through to **Closed Won** or **Closed Lost**.

## Emailing a contact

From a contact's record, use the email action to send a message directly from Okleevo. Message templates are available to speed up common outreach.
