## What's the difference between "business" and "individual" contact type?

It's how you categorise the contact — a business contact usually represents a company relationship, while an individual is a single person you deal with directly. Use whichever matches how you think about the relationship.

## Can my whole team see the same contacts?

Yes — contacts are shared across your business, so your whole team sees the same up-to-date record and communication history for each client.

## Where do emails I send from the CRM actually come from?

They're sent directly through Okleevo's own email engine, so there's no need to connect a separate mailbox or third-party email tool.

## What happens if I move a contact to "Closed Lost" by mistake?

Just open the contact and change their pipeline stage again — stages can be updated at any time.

## Can I see all my communication with a contact in one place?

Yes — the **Communication** tab on each contact's record shows their email history, filterable by sent or received.
