title: CRM Overview
summary: A centralized hub for your leads and customers.
---

The CRM module is where you manage every lead and customer relationship in one place — contact details, pipeline stage, communication history, and internal notes.

## What it's for

- Keeping a single record for every contact: name, email, phone, company, and type
- Tracking each contact through your sales pipeline
- Sending branded emails directly to contacts without leaving Okleevo
- Logging notes and reviewing communication history per contact

## Pipeline stages

Every contact sits in one of six stages:

- **New**
- **Contacted**
- **Proposal**
- **Negotiation**
- **Closed Won**
- **Closed Lost**

## Who it's for

Anyone responsible for sales, account management, or customer relationships who needs visibility into where every deal or client stands.
