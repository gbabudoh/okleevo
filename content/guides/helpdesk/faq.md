## What's the difference between "Resolved" and "Closed"?

**Resolved** means the issue is fixed but you're keeping the ticket open in case the customer has follow-up questions. **Closed** means the matter is fully done.

## Can I change a ticket's priority after creating it?

Yes — priority isn't locked in; update it as you learn more about how urgent the issue actually is.

## What does the AI Assist button do on the ticket description?

It sends your draft description to an AI writing assistant, which can improve the grammar and clarity, make the tone more professional or friendly, shorten it, expand it, or summarise it — the result replaces your draft, and you can always edit further before saving.

## Will customers see my internal notes or reply drafts?

Only what you actually send as a reply becomes part of the customer-facing conversation — always review your reply before sending.

## How do I find a specific ticket quickly?

Use the search bar on the Helpdesk page to search by subject, customer name, or email.
