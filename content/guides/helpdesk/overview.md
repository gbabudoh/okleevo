---
title: Helpdesk Overview
summary: A ticket system for organising and resolving customer support requests.
---

The Helpdesk module organises every customer support request into tickets, so nothing gets lost and you can track each issue from open to resolved.

## What it's for

- Creating support tickets for customer issues, questions, or requests
- Setting priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent) and category (General Support, Technical Issue, Billing, Feature Request, Bug Report)
- Replying to tickets and keeping a full conversation history
- Tracking each ticket through its lifecycle: Open → Pending → In Progress → Resolved → Closed

## Who it's for

Support teams and business owners who handle customer questions and need an organised, searchable record of every request.
