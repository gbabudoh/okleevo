title: Helpdesk User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for creating and managing tickets.
---

## Creating a ticket

1. Click **New Ticket**.
2. Fill in the subject, customer name, and customer email.
3. Set a **Priority** (Low, Medium, High, Urgent) and a **Category** (General Support, Technical Issue, Billing, Feature Request, Bug Report).
4. Write a description of the issue.
5. Click the **AI Assist** button above the description field if you'd like help improving the wording, fixing grammar, making it more professional, or summarising a longer explanation.
6. Click **Create Ticket**.

## Replying to a ticket

1. Open the ticket from your ticket list.
2. Type your reply in the box at the bottom of the ticket detail view and press **Enter** to send (or click the send button).
3. Every reply is logged in the ticket's conversation history, along with who sent it and when.

## Managing ticket status

As you work a ticket, its status moves through: **Open** → **Pending** → **In Progress** → **Resolved** → **Closed**. Update the status to reflect where things stand so your team has an accurate view of what still needs attention.
