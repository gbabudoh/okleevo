## Tutorial: Handling a new customer issue end-to-end

1. Create a ticket as soon as the issue comes in, setting the right priority so urgent issues aren't missed.
2. Use **AI Assist** on the description if the customer's original message needs cleaning up or summarising before your team works from it.
3. Reply to the customer as you investigate, keeping the conversation in the ticket rather than a side channel.
4. Move the ticket to **Resolved** once the issue is fixed, then **Closed** once you've confirmed the customer is satisfied.

## Tutorial: Triaging urgent issues quickly

1. Sort or filter your ticket list by priority.
2. Address **Urgent** and **High** priority tickets first.
3. Use categories (e.g. Bug Report vs General Support) to route issues to the right person on your team.

## Tutorial: Writing a clear, professional ticket description fast

1. When creating a ticket, jot down the issue quickly, even if the wording is rough.
2. Click **AI Assist** above the description field and choose an action — **Improve writing** to tidy it up, **Make professional** for a more polished tone, or **Summarize** if the customer's original message was long.
3. Review the result before saving — AI suggestions are a starting point, not a replacement for your own judgement.
