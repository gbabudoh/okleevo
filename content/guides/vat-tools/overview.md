VAT Tools is a quick, standalone calculator for adding or removing VAT on any amount — separate from the full VAT return preparation found in the Taxation module.

## What it's for

- Adding VAT to a net amount (e.g. quoting a price to a customer)
- Removing VAT from a gross amount (e.g. working out the VAT-exclusive cost of a receipt)
- Quickly applying the Standard (20%), Reduced (5%), or Zero (0%) rate, or a custom rate
- Keeping a running history of your recent calculations in this session

## Who it's for

Anyone who needs a fast, precise VAT calculation on the fly — for a quote, an invoice check, or working out what a receipt actually cost before VAT.
