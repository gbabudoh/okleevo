summary: Step-by-step instructions for adding, removing, and reviewing VAT calculations.
---

## Adding VAT to an amount

1. Make sure **Add VAT** is selected in the Calculator card.
2. Enter the **Net Amount (excl. VAT)**.
3. Choose a VAT rate — 20% Standard, 5% Reduced, 0% Zero, or **Custom Rate** for anything else.
4. Click **Calculate**. The Summary panel shows the Net Amount, VAT Amount, and Gross Amount.

## Removing VAT from an amount

1. Switch to **Remove VAT**.
2. Enter the **Gross Amount (incl. VAT)** — the total you actually paid or charged.
3. Choose the rate that applied, and click **Calculate** to see the VAT-exclusive net amount.

## Using the quick-reference rate cards

Click any of the **Current UK VAT Rates** cards (Standard, Reduced, Zero) to apply that rate to the calculator instantly.

## Reviewing and exporting your history

Every calculation you run is added to **Recent Calculations**. Use **Export** to download your session's history as a CSV, or **Clear History** to reset it.
