## Tutorial: Quoting a customer a VAT-inclusive price

1. Select **Add VAT**.
2. Enter your net price and select the Standard 20% rate (or whichever applies).
3. Use the **Gross Amount** result as the price you quote the customer.

## Tutorial: Working out the real cost of a business receipt

1. Select **Remove VAT**.
2. Enter the total amount shown on the receipt (the gross, VAT-inclusive amount).
3. The **Net Amount** result is what the purchase actually cost before VAT — useful for expense records or budgeting.

## Tutorial: Comparing rates quickly

1. Run the same amount through the calculator at 20%, then again at 5%, using the quick-reference rate cards to switch rates.
2. Compare the VAT Amount results side by side in your **Recent Calculations** history.
