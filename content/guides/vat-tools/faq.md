summary: Common questions and troubleshooting for VAT Tools.

## Is this the same as preparing my VAT return?

No — VAT Tools is a quick standalone calculator. To prepare an actual quarterly VAT return with Box 1-9 figures, use the **VAT** tab inside the Taxation module.

## Does my calculation history save permanently?

No — history here is kept only for your current browser session. If you need a permanent record, export it to CSV before closing the tab or clearing your browser data.

## What rate should I use for a custom rate?

Enter whatever rate actually applies to your specific goods or services if it isn't 20%, 5%, or 0% — Okleevo will calculate using exactly the rate you enter.

## Why did my history disappear?

Either you clicked **Clear History**, or you started a new browser session — this calculator's history isn't stored in the database, unlike your invoices and expenses.
