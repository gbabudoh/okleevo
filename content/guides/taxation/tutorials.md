## Tutorial: Preparing for your accountant at year-end

1. Go through each relevant tab (Corporation Tax, Self Assessment, VAT, PAYE) and make sure your figures reflect the current period.
2. Download a report from each tab you use.
3. Send the downloaded reports to your accountant — they handle the actual filing with HMRC.

## Tutorial: Quarterly VAT return

1. At the end of each VAT quarter, go to the **VAT** tab.
2. Check the **Current Quarter** panel for your Output VAT and Input VAT totals.
3. Click **Prepare VAT Return**, confirm your sales and purchase figures, and download the report before your submission deadline (1 month + 7 days after the quarter ends).

## Tutorial: Estimating your Self Assessment bill mid-year

1. Go to the **Self Assessment** tab and select the current tax year.
2. Enter your income sources as they come in throughout the year, rather than waiting until January.
3. Check the **Total Tax Due** figure regularly so there are no surprises when the filing deadline approaches.
