The Taxation module helps you calculate and prepare your UK tax figures across five areas: **Self Assessment**, **Corporation Tax**, **PAYE & NI**, **VAT**, and **Capital Gains**.

> **Important:** Okleevo is not directly connected to HMRC. This module calculates and prepares your figures — you download the reports and share them with your accountant (or file them yourself) for official submission to HMRC.

## What it's for

- Calculating Corporation Tax using current UK rates and Marginal Relief
- Preparing Self Assessment figures across income sources (self-employment, employment, property, dividends)
- Calculating PAYE & National Insurance for your payroll
- Preparing VAT returns (Box 1–9 figures)
- Estimating Capital Gains Tax on asset disposals
- Downloading everything as a PDF or Excel report to hand to your accountant

## Who it's for

Business owners and bookkeepers who want to see an accurate estimate of what they owe before their accountant finalises and files it.
