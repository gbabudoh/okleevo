title: Taxation User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for each tax calculator.
---

## Corporation Tax

1. Go to the **Corporation Tax** tab.
2. Choose your accounting period and enter your taxable profit.
3. Okleevo applies the current UK rules automatically: 19% up to $50,000, 25% over $250,000, with Marginal Relief calculated in between.
4. Click **Generate CT600** to download a report for your accountant.

## Self Assessment

1. Go to the **Self Assessment** tab and select the tax year.
2. Click into each income source card (Self-Employment, Employment, Property, Dividends & Interest) to enter your figures.
3. Add your allowable expenses.
4. Okleevo calculates your taxable income and total tax + NI due automatically.
5. Use **Start New Return** to begin preparing a return, or **Download SA100** to export your figures.

## PAYE & NI

1. Go to the **PAYE & NI** tab to see your current payroll tax position, based on your active employees' salaries.
2. Use the **PAYE Calculator** for a one-off calculation on a specific salary.
3. Use **Download RTI Report** to prepare a Full Payment Submission summary for your accountant.

## VAT

1. Go to the **VAT** tab to see your current quarter's Output VAT, Input VAT, and Net VAT due.
2. Click **Prepare VAT Return** to enter your sales and purchase totals and generate the Box 1/4/5 breakdown.
3. Download the result as a PDF or Excel report.

## Capital Gains

1. Go to the **Capital Gains** tab and click **Calculate CGT**.
2. Enter the disposal value, acquisition cost, and any allowable expenses.
3. Okleevo applies the Annual Exempt Amount and the correct rate for your asset type.

## Downloading reports

Every tab has a **Download** or **Export** option that produces a PDF or Excel file, ready to send to your accountant or keep for your own records.
