Suppliers is a directory for the vendors you buy from — store their contact details, message them directly, send purchase orders by email, and track order counts and ratings.

## What it's for

- Keeping one record per supplier: contact person, email, phone, category, payment terms, and lead time
- Emailing a supplier directly from their record
- Sending a purchase order by email, with an auto-generated reference number
- Rating suppliers out of 5 stars as your relationship develops

## Categories

Suppliers are grouped into 13 categories — Raw Materials, Manufacturing, Packaging, Logistics, Services, Equipment & Tooling, Facilities & Utilities, IT & Software, Marketing & Sales, Quality & Compliance, HR & Staffing, Finance & Legal, and After-sales & Support.

## Stats at a glance

The header shows total Suppliers (with an active count), Total Spent, total Orders placed, and your Average Rating.

## Who it's for

Anyone managing purchasing or vendor relationships who wants a simple record of who to order from and how to reach them.
