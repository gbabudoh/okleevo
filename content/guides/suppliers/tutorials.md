## Tutorial: Onboarding a new supplier

1. Click **Add Supplier** and fill in their company name, category, contact person, and email — these are the essentials to get them into your directory.
2. Add their payment terms and lead time so you have that on hand next time you need to place an order.
3. Once you've worked with them a little, come back and rate them out of 5 stars.

## Tutorial: Placing a restock order

1. Open the supplier you want to order from and click **Order**.
2. Enter the quantity you need and set the priority (Standard, Expedited, or Critical) based on urgency.
3. Click **Place Order** — the supplier receives a formatted purchase order email with a reference number, and the order is logged against their record.

## Tutorial: Following up with a supplier

1. Open the supplier's record and click **Message**.
2. Set a Priority so the urgency is clear, write your subject and message, and send — it goes out as a real email to their listed address, branded as coming from Okleevo.

## Tutorial: Reviewing your supplier base

1. Use the category pills to look at one type of supplier at a time (e.g. Logistics).
2. Sort mentally by the Average Rating and Total Spent stats to see who you rely on most.
3. Export the full list to CSV if you need to share it outside Okleevo.
