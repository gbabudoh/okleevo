---
title: Suppliers User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for managing suppliers and orders.
---

## Adding a supplier

1. Click **Add Supplier**.
2. Fill in Company name (required), Category, Contact person, Email (required), Phone, Website, Payment terms (Net 30/60/90 or Prepayment), and Lead time (e.g. "7-10 days").
3. Click **Add supplier**.

## Viewing a supplier

Click a card or row to open the detail view — Total Spent and Total Orders, a star rating you can click to update, Contact info, Location, Operations details (Lead Time, Payment Terms), and Message/Order/Delete actions in the footer.

## Rating a supplier

Inside a supplier's detail view, click a star (1–5) to rate them — this saves immediately, no separate save step needed.

## Messaging a supplier

Click **Message** on a card or in the detail view, choose a Priority (Low/Normal/High/Urgent), write a Subject and Message, and send — this emails the supplier directly at their listed address.

## Placing an order

Click **Order** (or use the "⋮" menu → Place Order), enter a Quantity and choose a Priority (Standard/Expedited/Critical), then click **Place Order**. Okleevo generates a purchase order reference, emails the supplier a formatted purchase order, and records the order against that supplier's order count.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search box (matches name, contact, or email), the category pills, and the status filter (Active/Inactive/Pending/Suspended) to narrow the list.

## Exporting

Click the header **Export** icon to download a CSV of all your suppliers, including contact details, category, status, rating, orders, and spend.

## Deleting a supplier

Use the "⋮" menu or the detail view's Delete button, then confirm. This is permanent.
