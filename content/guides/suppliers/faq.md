---
title: Suppliers FAQ
summary: Common questions and troubleshooting for Suppliers.
---

## Why does filtering by "Pending" or "Suspended" always show no results?

New suppliers are always created as Active, and there's currently no way to move one into a Pending or Suspended state — only Active and Inactive are actually used today.

## Why does a supplier's performance bar always show 0%?

That performance score is built from on-time delivery, quality, response time, and price competitiveness — none of which are tracked yet, so it always shows as 0% for every supplier.

## Does placing an order update "Total Spent"?

It increases that supplier's order count, but it doesn't add anything to Total Spent — there's no field in the Place Order form for the order value.

## Why is Minimum Order always shown as $0, or the City/Country always blank?

Those fields are displayed in the supplier detail view but there's currently no input anywhere to set them, so they'll always show as empty or zero.

## Where does a purchase order actually get recorded?

The only record of a purchase order today is the email sent to the supplier — there's no separate purchase-order list or history to browse afterward.

## Can my whole team see the same supplier records?

Yes — suppliers are shared across your business, so everyone sees the same contact details, ratings, and order history.
