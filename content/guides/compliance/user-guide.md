## Creating a compliance item

1. Click **New Entry** (or **Add Item** from the Items tab).
2. Enter a Title (required), Description, Category, Priority, and who it's Assigned To (defaults to "General Team").
3. Click **Create Item**. New items are automatically due 30 days out — there's no due-date picker in the create form yet, so adjust the date afterward if you need a different deadline.

## Browsing items

Switch to the **Items** tab, then use the search box, category pills, and Grid/List toggle to find what you're looking for. Each item shows its priority, a risk bar, due date (highlighted if overdue), and assigned owner.

## Viewing an item's details

Click any item to see its status, priority, risk level, description, key dates, and who's responsible for it.

## Marking an item complete

Open the item and click **Mark Compliant**. This saves immediately and is reflected the next time the list refreshes.

## Duplicating, exporting, and deleting an item

From an item's "⋮" menu: **Duplicate** creates a copy for this session (it won't survive a refresh, so finish setting it up and save any changes to it while you have it), **Export** downloads that single item as a file, and **Delete** removes it permanently after you confirm.

## Exporting a full report

Click the header **Export** button, choose a format (PDF, Excel, or CSV), and generate a report listing all of your compliance items with their status, priority, due date, and description.

## The Overview, Frameworks, Risks, and Audits tabs

**Overview** shows your Compliance Score and a status breakdown computed from your real items. **Risks** shows risk counts and a risk-by-category breakdown, also computed from your real items. **Frameworks** and **Audits** are placeholders for now — there isn't yet a way to add a framework or log an audit through the UI.
