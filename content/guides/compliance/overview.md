Compliance Hub is where you track regulatory and compliance items — what's due, what's overdue, and how risky each item is — organised across five tabs: Overview, Items, Frameworks, Risks, and Audits.

## What it's for

- Creating and tracking compliance items with a category, priority, due date, and assigned owner
- Seeing your overall Compliance Score and a breakdown of Compliant/Pending/Overdue/At Risk items
- Filtering items by category: Data Privacy, Financial, Legal, Security, Operational, Environmental

## What's fully working today

Creating, listing, marking complete, and deleting compliance **items** are all backed by real, saved data. The Items tab is the core of this module.

## What's still a preview

The **Frameworks** and **Audits** tabs, and the detailed risk-metrics section on the **Risks** tab, don't have a way to add real data through the UI yet — they'll show as empty until that's built out. Treat compliance item tracking as the reliable part of this module for now.

## Who it's for

Anyone responsible for keeping track of regulatory deadlines and compliance obligations across the business.
