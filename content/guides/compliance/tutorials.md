## Tutorial: Logging a new regulatory obligation

1. Click **New Entry**, give it a clear title (e.g. "Annual data protection review"), pick the closest category (e.g. Data Privacy), and set a priority.
2. Assign it to whoever owns the obligation.
3. Once created, open the item and adjust its due date if 30 days out isn't accurate for this obligation.

## Tutorial: Running a weekly compliance check-in

1. Open the **Overview** tab and check your Compliance Score and the Overdue/At Risk counts.
2. Switch to the **Items** tab and filter by category to review anything due soon.
3. Mark anything you've completed as **Mark Compliant**.

## Tutorial: Producing a report for an auditor or manager

1. Click the header **Export** button.
2. Choose PDF, Excel, or CSV depending on what your audience needs.
3. Generate the report — it lists every compliance item with its status, priority, due date, and description.

## Tutorial: Reviewing risk by category

1. Open the **Risks** tab to see risk counts (High/Medium/Low) and a breakdown of average risk per category, both computed from your real items.
2. Use this to decide which category of obligations needs the most attention next.
