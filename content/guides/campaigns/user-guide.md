---
title: Campaigns User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for creating and reviewing campaigns.
---

## Creating a campaign

1. Click **New Campaign**.
2. Enter a Campaign name and Email subject (both required), choose a Type (Promotional, Newsletter, Announcement, Transactional), and set an Audience — either type your own or pick a preset pill (All Subscribers, VIP Customers, New Signups, Inactive).
3. Write your Content — a live character counter shows how long it is.
4. Submit to save the campaign.

## Browsing your campaigns

Use the search box (matches name, subject, or audience) and the Grid/List toggle to browse. Each card or row shows sent count, open %, click %, and revenue (if any).

## Viewing a report

Click **Reports** on a campaign (or click its row in List view) to see Open Rate, Click Rate, Revenue, and an Engagement Overview with delivery/open/click progress bars, plus its type, audience, creation date, and status.

## Deleting a campaign

Click the trash icon on a card or row, then confirm. This is permanent.
