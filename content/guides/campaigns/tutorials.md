## Tutorial: Planning a promotional campaign

1. Click **New Campaign**, name it, set Type to Promotional, and write a compelling subject line.
2. Pick an audience pill (e.g. All Subscribers) or type a custom description of who you're targeting.
3. Write your content, keeping an eye on the character counter, then save.

## Tutorial: Reviewing how a past campaign performed

1. Find the campaign in Grid or List view.
2. Click **Reports** to see its Open Rate, Click Rate, and Revenue, along with the Engagement Overview bars showing delivery, opens, and clicks relative to each other.

## Tutorial: Comparing several campaigns at a glance

1. Switch to List view for a compact table of every campaign's Sent, Open, Click, and Revenue figures side by side.
2. Use the search box to narrow down to a specific audience or subject line you're comparing.
