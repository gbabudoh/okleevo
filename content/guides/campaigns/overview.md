title: Campaigns Overview
summary: Create and track email marketing campaigns.
---

Campaigns is where you set up email marketing campaigns — name them, write a subject and content, target an audience segment, and review performance stats like open rate, click rate, and revenue.

## What it's for

- Creating a campaign with a name, type (Promotional, Newsletter, Announcement, or Transactional), subject line, audience, and content
- Reviewing sent/open/click stats and revenue per campaign in a Report view
- Searching and browsing your campaigns in Grid or List view

## Stats at a glance

The header shows Active campaigns, Total Reach (people sent to), Avg Open rate, and Revenue generated across all campaigns.

## Who it's for

Anyone planning and reviewing email marketing sends to their contact list.

## Good to know

Campaigns you create here are saved as drafts you can review and report on. Sending a campaign out to your contacts is handled by Okleevo's internal email engine and is subject to a safety limit of 100 recipients per send to protect deliverability — for larger lists, use a dedicated marketing send tool.
