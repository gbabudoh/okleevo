## How do I actually send a campaign to my contacts?

Sending isn't yet exposed as a button on this page — campaigns you create here are saved and ready for reporting, but triggering an actual send currently needs to go through Okleevo's backend send process rather than a button in this view. Ask your Okleevo contact if you need a campaign sent out.

## Why is there a 100-recipient limit?

Okleevo's internal email engine caps a single campaign send at 100 recipients to protect deliverability and avoid being flagged as spam. For larger lists, use a dedicated marketing email service.

## Does picking "VIP Customers" or another audience preset actually filter who receives the email?

The audience field is currently descriptive — it labels the campaign for your own reference. The actual recipient list, when a campaign is sent, is your contacts who have given email consent and haven't unsubscribed, regardless of the audience label chosen.

## Why do some campaigns show $0 revenue?

Revenue is only shown if it's been recorded against a campaign — there's no field in the campaign creation form for entering revenue or cost directly.

## How is "Avg Open" calculated across all my campaigns?

It's the average of each individual campaign's own open rate (opens ÷ sent), not a single combined total — so a campaign with no sends yet doesn't skew the average.
