---
title: E-Signature User Guide
summary: Step-by-step walkthrough of the signing experience.
---

## Uploading a document

Click the **Upload** icon in the header and choose a file — PDF, Word document, or an image, up to 10MB. You'll see a confirmation once it's picked.

## Starting a signature request

Click **New Signature Request** to open the Digital Signature modal. Choose how you'd like to sign:

- **Draw** — sign with your mouse or finger on the canvas; use **Clear** to start over.
- **Type** — type your name and it renders in a signature-style font.
- **Upload** — bring in an image of your signature.

Click **Adopt & Sign** to confirm.

## Filtering documents

Use the status pills — All, Signed, Pending, Draft, Declined, Expired — and the search box to narrow the document list. Switch between Grid and List view with the toggle.

## Viewing a document

Click a document card or row to open the viewer, which shows a "SIGNED" stamp for completed documents and gives you Export, Share, and Delete actions.

## Deleting a document

Use the delete action in the viewer or list, then confirm.
