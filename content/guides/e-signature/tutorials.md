## Tutorial: Previewing the full signing flow

1. Click **Upload** and choose a document under 10MB.
2. Click **New Signature Request** and try each signing method — draw a signature on the canvas, clear it and type your name instead, or try the upload option.
3. Click **Adopt & Sign** to see the confirmation.
4. Open the viewer for a signed-looking document to see the "SIGNED" stamp treatment.

## Tutorial: Exploring document filters

1. Use the status pills to see how documents would be organised across Signed, Pending, Draft, Declined, and Expired.
2. Try the search box and the Grid/List toggle to see both ways of browsing a document list.
