E-Signature is where document signing lives in Okleevo — upload a document, adopt a signature, and track status across Signed, Pending, Draft, Declined, and Expired.

## What it's for

- Uploading a document (PDF, Word, or image, up to 10MB)
- Adopting a signature by drawing it, typing your name in a signature font, or uploading an image
- Tracking documents by status, with search and category filters

## Current status

This module is an early preview. You can walk through the full signing experience — draw or type a signature, see a document marked as signed, and use the export/share/delete actions — but documents and signatures aren't saved to your account yet, so nothing you add here persists after you refresh or leave the page. Treat it as a look at how signing will work rather than a place to store real, legally-executed documents today.

## Who it's for

Anyone wanting to preview Okleevo's upcoming e-signature experience.
