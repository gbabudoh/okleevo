## Tutorial: Following up on a client email with full context

1. Open the client's email in your Inbox.
2. Check the client context panel that appears — it shows their pipeline stage, any recent invoices and their payment status, and any open support tickets.
3. Click **Reply** to respond directly, informed by what you just saw, or click **View in CRM** to dig deeper into their record.

## Tutorial: Sending a document by email

1. Click **Compose**.
2. Fill in the recipient, subject, and message.
3. Click **Attach files**, choose your document, and wait for the upload to finish.
4. Click **Send**.

## Tutorial: Keeping a tidy inbox with labels

1. As emails come in, open each one and assign a label — Marketing, Clients, or Priority — based on what it's about.
2. Use the Labels list in the sidebar to focus on just one category at a time, like reviewing all your Priority emails first thing in the morning.

## Tutorial: Cleaning up unwanted mail

1. Open a message you don't want.
2. Use the "⋯" menu to **Mark as Spam**, or click the trash icon to move it to Trash.
3. Check your Spam and Trash folders periodically in case anything needs recovering (there's no separate "restore" action — you'd need to forward or resend from there if needed).
