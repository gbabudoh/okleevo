title: Mail Engine FAQ
summary: Common questions and troubleshooting for Mail Engine.
---

## Is there a Drafts folder?

The folder list you can browse is Inbox, Sent, Spam, and Trash — there isn't a Drafts view in the current interface, so compose a message when you're ready to send it rather than saving it partway through.

## Where does the client context panel get its information from?

It matches the sender's email address against your CRM contacts and pulls their pipeline stage, recent invoices, and recent support tickets automatically — no manual linking required.

## What happens if I mark a message as Spam?

It moves to your Spam folder, out of your main Inbox view, without being deleted.

## Can I recover a message I deleted?

Deleted messages move to Trash rather than disappearing immediately — check there if you need to find something you removed by mistake.

## What file types can I attach when composing?

Common document, spreadsheet, and image formats are supported (e.g. PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, and standard image formats).

## Why do I see "Create Contact" instead of client details on some emails?

That means the sender's email address doesn't match anyone in your CRM yet — click it to quickly add them as a contact.
