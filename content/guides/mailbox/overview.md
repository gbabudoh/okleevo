Okleevo Mail Engine is your built-in business email client — read, send, organise, and label messages without connecting a separate mail provider.

## What it's for

- Browsing your **Inbox**, **Sent**, **Spam**, and **Trash** folders
- Composing new emails with file attachments
- Replying to or forwarding a message
- Labelling messages (Marketing, Clients, Priority) to keep your inbox organised
- Seeing CRM context for whoever emailed you — their pipeline stage, recent invoices, and recent support tickets — right next to the message

## Always available

Mail Engine appears in your sidebar regardless of which optional modules your business has enabled — it's a core part of Okleevo.

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants to handle business email and see the full picture of a client relationship without switching between apps.
