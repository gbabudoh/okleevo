---
title: Mail Engine User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for using your business email.
---

## Reading your mail

Use the folder list (Inbox, Sent, Spam, Trash) to browse messages. Click any message to open it — unread messages are marked in bold with a dot, and opening one automatically marks it as read.

## Composing a new email

1. Click **Compose**.
2. Fill in **To**, **Subject**, and your **Message**.
3. Optionally click **Attach files** to add one or more files.
4. Click **Send**.

## Replying and forwarding

Open a message and use **Reply** to respond to the sender (quoting the original message below your reply), or **Forward** to send it on to someone else.

## Labelling a message

Open a message and click the tag icon to assign it a label — **Marketing**, **Clients**, or **Priority** — or choose "No label" to clear it. Use the **Labels** list in the sidebar to filter your inbox down to just one label.

## Starring, deleting, and marking as spam

From an open message: click the star icon to flag it, the trash icon to move it to Trash, or use the "⋯" menu for **Mark as Spam**.

## Searching

Use the search box to filter the current folder by subject or sender.

## Seeing client context

When you open a message, Okleevo automatically looks up the sender in your CRM. If there's a match, you'll see their name, company, pipeline stage, recent invoices (with status), and recent support tickets, plus a **View in CRM** link. If there's no match, you'll see a **Create Contact** shortcut instead.

## Refreshing your inbox

Click the refresh icon in the header to re-sync your messages.
