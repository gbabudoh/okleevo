summary: Collaborative project management with a Kanban board and subtasks.

Tasks is a Kanban-style task manager for tracking work — from quick to-dos to multi-step projects with subtasks, priority, due dates, and an assignee.

## What it's for

- Organising work into four stages: **To Do**, **In Progress**, **Review**, and **Done**
- Breaking a task down into a checklist of subtasks and tracking completion progress
- Assigning tasks to a person and setting a due date and priority
- Optionally linking a task to a Project

## Views

Switch between a **Grid** view (Kanban board with four columns) and a **List** view (a compact table) using the toggle above the task list.

## Stats at a glance

The header shows Total Tasks, Completed, Subtasks (completed vs. total), and overall Progress — all updating live as you work.

## Who it's for

Anyone managing day-to-day work items or a project's task list, especially teams that want a visual, drag-free Kanban board.
