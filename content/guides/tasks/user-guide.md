title: Tasks User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for managing tasks, subtasks, and status.
---

## Creating a task

1. Click **New Task** (or the **+ Add Task** option in a column's "⋯" menu, or the floating action button on mobile).
2. Fill in the Title (required), Description, Priority, Due date, Assigned to (a plain name — there's no user picker), and optionally a Project.
3. Click **Create Task**. The task appears at the top of the **To Do** column.

## Moving a task between stages

There are two ways to change a task's status:

- **Quick move** — on a task card, use the small arrow buttons to step it one column forward or back.
- **Detailed move** — open the task, pick a status from the 4-button selector inside the detail view. This only *stages* the change (shown with a dashed border) — click **Apply** next to Status to actually save it.

To finish a task in one click, open it and use the **Mark as Done** button in the footer — this applies immediately, without the stage/apply step.

## Working with subtasks

Open a task to see its Subtasks checklist:

- Type into **Add subtask…** and press Enter (or click **+**) to add one.
- Tick a subtask's checkbox to mark it complete — this updates the task's progress percentage automatically.
- Click the trash icon next to a subtask to remove it.

## Editing a task

Open the task, click the pencil icon next to the title to enter edit mode, update the title/description, then click **Save**.

## Filtering and searching

Use the search box to match a task's title or description, and the **Status**, **Priority**, and **Assignee** filters to narrow the list. The Assignee filter only appears once at least one task has been assigned to someone.

## Sorting and clearing a column

Each column's "⋯" menu offers **Sort by Priority** (reorders Urgent → High → Medium → Low) and **Clear Completed** (hides tasks in that column that are 100% complete). Both only affect what you see in the current session — refreshing the page brings cleared/reordered tasks back to their original state.

## Duplicating and deleting

Use a card's "⋯" menu to **Duplicate** a task (creates a copy titled "… (Copy)") or **Delete** it. Deleting asks for confirmation and removes the task and all of its subtasks permanently.
