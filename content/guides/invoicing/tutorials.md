## Tutorial: Bill a client from start to finish

1. **Create** the invoice with your line items and a due date.
2. **Send** it to the client by email directly from the invoice.
3. **Wait for payment** — the invoice stays in **Sent** status until you mark it paid.
4. **Mark as paid** once the client pays, so your records and any connected reports (like Cashflow) stay accurate.
5. If the due date passes with no payment, the invoice will show as **Overdue** — this is a good trigger to follow up with the client.

## Tutorial: Chasing an overdue invoice

1. Filter your invoice list by the **Overdue** status to see everything that needs following up.
2. Open the invoice and re-send it as a polite reminder.
3. Keep the client's contact details up to date so reminders reach the right inbox.

## Tutorial: Keeping your books tidy at month-end

1. Filter by **Paid** to see everything settled this period.
2. Use the export option to download the list for your accountant or your own records.
3. Cross-check any invoices still in **Draft** — these haven't been sent yet and won't appear in your income figures.
