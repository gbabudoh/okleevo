## Creating an invoice

1. Click **New Invoice** in the top-right of the Invoicing page (or the floating **+** button on mobile).
2. Fill in the client details.
3. Add one or more line items — each has a description, quantity, and rate. Use **Add Item** to add more rows.
4. Set a due date.
5. Review the total, then save the invoice as a draft.

## Sending an invoice

- Open the invoice and use the **Send email** action (mail icon), or use the same action from the invoice list's row menu.
- The invoice is emailed directly to the client from Okleevo — you don't need a separate email client.
- Once sent, the invoice's status automatically updates to **Sent**.

## Tracking status

Each invoice carries one of five statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Created but not yet sent |
| Sent | Emailed to the client, awaiting payment |
| Paid | Payment received |
| Overdue | Past its due date and still unpaid |
| Cancelled | No longer active |

Use the **Filter** control above the invoice list to narrow the list down to a specific status.

## Exporting

Use the export menu (top of the invoice list) to download your invoices for bookkeeping or to share with your accountant.
