title: Invoicing FAQ
summary: Common questions and troubleshooting for Invoicing.
---

## Can I edit an invoice after I've sent it?

You can still edit the invoice record, but the client already received the version you sent — if the amount or details change, re-send it so the client has the corrected copy.

## What happens if sending the email fails?

If the send fails, the invoice stays in its previous status (it won't be marked **Sent**). Check the client's email address for typos and try again.

## How do I know which invoices still need chasing?

Use the **Filter** control to show only **Overdue** invoices — this is the fastest way to see what needs a follow-up.

## Where do my invoice figures show up elsewhere in Okleevo?

Paid invoices feed into your Cashflow and Taxation figures, since they represent real income. Draft invoices don't count until they're actually sent and settled.

## Who can see my invoices?

Invoices are scoped to your business — only members of your organisation can view or manage them.
