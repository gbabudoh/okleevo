The Invoicing module lets you create professional invoices, send them directly to clients by email, and track their status from draft through to payment.

## What it's for

- Building line-item invoices with descriptions, quantities, and rates
- Sending invoices straight to a client's inbox without leaving Okleevo
- Tracking each invoice's status: **Draft**, **Sent**, **Paid**, **Overdue**, or **Cancelled**
- Filtering and exporting your invoice list for bookkeeping or your accountant

## Who it's for

Anyone who bills clients — freelancers, agencies, and small businesses that need to look professional and get paid on time without juggling a separate invoicing tool.
