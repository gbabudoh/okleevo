---
title: Booking User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for managing appointments.
---

## Creating a booking

1. Click **New Booking**.
2. Fill in Client name, Email, Service, Date, and Time (all required). Optionally add Phone, Duration (30/45/60/90 minutes or 2 hours), Type (Video Call, Phone Call, or In-Person), Location (only enterable if Type is In-Person), and Notes.
3. Click **Create Booking**. New bookings always start with status **Pending**.

## Viewing a booking

Click **Details** on any booking to see the client's contact info, service, date/time and duration, location (if in-person), and any notes.

## Updating a booking

Click **Edit** to change any field, including moving its **Status** forward — Pending → Confirmed → Completed, or Cancelled if it falls through.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search box to match a client name or service, and the status dropdown to narrow the list to just Confirmed, Pending, Completed, or Cancelled bookings.

## Deleting a booking

Click **Delete**, then confirm. This is permanent.
