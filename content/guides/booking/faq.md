title: Booking FAQ
summary: Common questions and troubleshooting for Booking.
---

## Why does every new booking start as "Pending"?

New bookings always start as Pending — there's no way to create one as already Confirmed. Open it afterward and use Edit to move it to Confirmed once it's locked in.

## Is there a calendar or day view?

Not currently — Booking is a searchable, filterable list sorted by date and time rather than a calendar grid.

## Why is the Location field greyed out?

Location only becomes enterable when the appointment Type is set to In-Person — it's not needed for Video Call or Phone Call appointments.

## What counts toward "Upcoming" in the stats row?

Anything that's Confirmed or Pending — Completed and Cancelled bookings aren't counted as upcoming.

## Can I change the date or time after creating a booking?

Yes — open **Edit** and update the date, time, or duration; the appointment's schedule updates accordingly.
