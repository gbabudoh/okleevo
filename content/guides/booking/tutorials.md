## Tutorial: Booking a new client consultation

1. Click **New Booking** and fill in the client's name, email, and the service they're booking.
2. Set the date and time, and pick Video Call, Phone Call, or In-Person as the type — add a Location if it's in-person.
3. Click **Create Booking** — it's saved as Pending until you confirm it.

## Tutorial: Confirming and completing an appointment

1. Once you've confirmed the appointment with the client, open it and use **Edit** to set Status to Confirmed.
2. After the appointment happens, edit it again and set Status to Completed so it's reflected correctly in your stats.

## Tutorial: Finding today's bookings

1. Use the search box if you know the client's name or service.
2. Otherwise, filter by status (e.g. Confirmed) to see everything that's locked in, since the list doesn't have a calendar/day view — it's a flat list sorted by date and time.

## Tutorial: Handling a cancellation

1. Open the booking and click **Edit**.
2. Change Status to Cancelled — this removes it from your "Upcoming" count while keeping a record that it existed.
