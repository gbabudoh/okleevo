Booking is where you create and manage client appointments — consultations, calls, and meetings — and keep track of their status from first request through to completion.

## What it's for

- Recording a booking's client details, service, date, time, duration, and type (Video Call, Phone Call, or In-Person)
- Tracking each booking's status: Pending, Confirmed, Completed, or Cancelled
- Searching and filtering your booking list by client, service, or status

## Stats at a glance

The header shows Total Bookings, Confirmed, Upcoming (confirmed plus pending), and Completed.

## Who it's for

Anyone managing a schedule of client appointments who wants a simple list-based view of what's coming up and what's been done.
