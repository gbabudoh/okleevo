## Tutorial: Logging a receipt on the go

1. Take a photo of the receipt (or have the PDF ready).
2. Click **Add Expense**, upload the receipt, and fill in the amount, date, and category.
3. Save — the expense and its receipt are now stored together, so you'll never have to dig through a shoebox of paper receipts again.

## Tutorial: Tracking spending on a specific project

1. When adding or editing an expense, select the relevant project from the **Project** dropdown.
2. Over time, this lets you see exactly how much a project cost — useful for project profitability reviews.

## Tutorial: Month-end expense review

1. Use the **Filter** control to review spending one category at a time (e.g. Travel, then Software).
2. Check the **Total Spent**, **Avg per Item**, and **Top Category** stats at the top of the page for a quick overview.
3. Export the list to share with your accountant or for your own records.
