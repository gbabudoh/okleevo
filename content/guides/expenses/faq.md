## Do I have to attach a receipt?

No — the receipt upload is optional, but attaching one is good practice in case you need proof of purchase later (for your accountant or in an HMRC enquiry).

## What file types can I upload for a receipt?

Image files and PDFs are supported.

## Why is "Top Category" showing "No data"?

That stat is based on your recorded expenses — once you've added a few, it will show whichever category you've spent the most in.

## Can I link an expense to a project after I've already saved it?

Yes — open the expense and update the **Project** field at any time.

## Where does my expense data show up elsewhere in Okleevo?

Your expenses feed into Cashflow and Taxation (as allowable expenses and VAT input), so keeping them accurate here keeps those figures accurate too.
