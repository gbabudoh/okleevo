The Expenses module lets you record every business expense, attach a receipt, and categorise your spending so your books stay accurate and ready for tax time.

## What it's for

- Recording expenses with title, amount, date, and category
- Attaching a photo or PDF of the receipt
- Categorising spending (Office, Software, Meals, Travel, Marketing, Rent, Utilities, Other)
- Linking an expense to a specific project, when relevant
- Searching, filtering, and exporting your expense history

## Who it's for

Anyone who needs to track what the business spends money on — whether that's a founder keeping their own receipts or a bookkeeper reconciling the whole team's spending.
