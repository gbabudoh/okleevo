title: Expenses User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for recording and managing expenses.
---

## Adding an expense

1. Click **Add Expense** (or **Add first expense** if your list is empty).
2. Optionally drag and drop a receipt image or PDF into the upload area, or click it to browse for a file.
3. Fill in the **Title** (e.g. "Office Supplies"), **Amount**, **Date**, and **Category**.
4. If the expense relates to a specific project, select it from the **Project** dropdown — otherwise leave it as "No project".
5. Click **Save Expense**.

## Editing an expense

Click any expense in the **Recent Expenses** list to open it, make your changes, and save.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search bar to find an expense by title, and the **Filter** control to narrow the list down to a specific category.

## Exporting your expenses

Use the **Export** button in the top-right to download your (filtered) expense list as a CSV file — useful for your own records or to hand to an accountant.
