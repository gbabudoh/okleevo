## Tutorial: A weekly check-in routine

1. Open the KPI Dashboard and glance at the **Overall Score** and **Trending Up** tiles for a quick read on direction.
2. Check the **Needs Attention** panel for any metric under 90% of its target, and click into its **Analysis** view for auto-generated recommendations.
3. Use **Sync Data** (or Refresh) to make sure you're looking at the latest numbers before a meeting.

## Tutorial: Reporting revenue trends to a stakeholder

1. Switch the Performance Trends chart to the **Revenue** tab to see the full 12-month bar chart.
2. Click the header **Download** icon to export the current metrics to CSV for inclusion in a report.

## Tutorial: Following a metric back to its source

1. Find the category card for the area you care about (e.g. Sales) and click **View All**.
2. You'll land on the module that generates that data (CRM, in this example) so you can investigate the individual records behind the number.

## Tutorial: Understanding a target

Remember targets are computed automatically — 10% above your current performance for growth metrics, or 10% below current spend for Operating Expenses — so a metric sitting at 100% of target simply reflects steady performance, not a fixed goal you set.
