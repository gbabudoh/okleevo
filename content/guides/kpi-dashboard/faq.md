## Why did my edits to a KPI disappear after I refreshed the page?

The dashboard always recomputes its 7 metrics live from your actual invoices, expenses, and contacts. Add/Edit/Delete on a card only changes what you see on screen for that session — it isn't a way to correct or override the underlying data.

## Where do these numbers actually come from?

Total Revenue, Net Profit Margin, and Operating Expenses come from your Invoices and Expenses; Total Sales Volume and Average Order Value come from your invoice records; Lead Acquisition and Customer Base come from your CRM contacts (by lead/customer status).

## How is "% change" calculated?

It compares this month's value to last month's for the same metric. If there was no data last month, a metric with any current value shows as a 100% increase.

## Can I add a completely custom KPI that isn't one of the 7 built-in metrics?

The "Add KPI" button lets you add a card to your current view, but it isn't connected to a live data source — the 7 metrics that pull from your real business data are fixed.

## Why does the time-range selector (Today/This Week/etc.) seem to show the same numbers?

The metrics themselves are always based on the last 12 months of data — the selector doesn't currently change which period is being calculated.
