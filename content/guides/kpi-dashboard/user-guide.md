## Reading the summary tiles

At the top you'll see four rollups: **Overall Score** (average progress-to-target across your metrics), **Trending Up** (how many metrics improved since last month), **On Target** (how many are at 90%+ of their target), and **Active KPIs** (total metric count).

## Filtering by category

Use the category pills — All, Financial, Sales, Marketing, Customer — to narrow the metric cards shown. Each pill shows a live count.

## Switching views

Toggle between **Grid** (cards with sparklines) and **List** view using the control above the metric cards.

## Digging into a metric

Each card's "⋮" menu offers:
- **Edit** — try out different values on screen (doesn't change your real data).
- **Sync Data** — refetches the latest computed metrics from your business data (same as the Refresh button).
- **Analysis** — shows the metric's 12-month trend plus auto-generated "Strengths" and "Recommendations" notes.
- **Delete** — removes the card from view for this session only.

## Performance Trends chart

Below the metric cards, switch between the **Revenue**, **Sales**, and **Customers** tabs to see that metric's full 12-month bar chart with month-by-month tooltips.

## Exporting

Click the **Download** icon in the header to export the currently filtered metrics as a CSV (name, category, value, target, % change, % progress).

## Jumping to the source data

Each category card at the bottom has a **View All** link that takes you to the module behind those numbers — Financial → Accounting, Sales → CRM, Marketing → Campaigns, Customer → Helpdesk.
