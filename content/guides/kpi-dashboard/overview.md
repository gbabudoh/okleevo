title: KPI Dashboard Overview
summary: A live, auto-computed view of your key business metrics.
---

The KPI Dashboard is a read-only, automatically computed view of 7 core business metrics — pulled straight from your real invoices, expenses, and contacts — with trend charts and target-progress bars.

## What it's for

- Seeing Total Revenue, Net Profit Margin, Operating Expenses, Total Sales Volume, Average Order Value, Lead Acquisition, and Customer Base at a glance
- Tracking each metric's month-over-month change and a 12-month trend sparkline
- Spotting Top Performers and metrics that Need Attention

## Categories

Metrics are grouped into four categories — Financial, Sales, Marketing, and Customer — each filterable via the category pills, with a "View All" link through to the related module (Accounting, CRM, Campaigns, or Helpdesk).

## How targets work

Each metric's target is set automatically as 10% above its current performance (or 10% below current spend, for expenses) — so targets adjust as your business grows rather than being fixed numbers you set yourself.

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants a quick, real-time snapshot of business performance without building their own reports.

## Good to know

The dashboard recalculates from your live data every time you open or refresh the page. The **Add/Edit/Delete KPI** actions let you experiment with the numbers shown on screen, but they don't change your underlying data — refreshing the page always restores the real, computed values.
