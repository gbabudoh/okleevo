title: HR Records FAQ
summary: Common questions and troubleshooting for HR Records.
---

## Does the "Message" button actually notify the employee?

No — it shows a confirmation on screen but doesn't send anything. Use your normal email or chat tool to actually reach the employee; treat this as a personal note-to-self rather than a delivery mechanism.

## Why is Average Rating always 0.0?

Performance ratings aren't currently tracked against employee records, so this stat has nothing to average yet.

## Why do I have to type "DELETE" to remove someone?

It's a safeguard against accidentally deleting an employee record — since removal is permanent, typing the word confirms you meant to do it.

## Can I set an employee's status to "Probation"?

Not currently — employee status is Active, On Leave, or Inactive (used for anyone who's left).

## Can I add skills, education, or emergency contact details?

Those fields exist on an employee's profile view but there's currently no form to fill them in, so they won't show anything for employees added through the current interface.

## Is an employee's location shown accurately?

Country always shows as "UK" and city is left blank — there's no field to set a more specific location today.
