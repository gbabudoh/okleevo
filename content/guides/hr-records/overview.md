HR Records is where you keep a record of every employee — their role, department, employment type, salary, and start date — organised by department.

## What it's for

- Adding a record for each employee: name, email, phone, job title, department, employment type, start date, salary, and reporting manager
- Browsing your team by department, with live counts per department
- Viewing a profile for any employee, including tenure and location
- Exporting your full employee list to CSV

## Departments

Records are organised across 12 departments: Engineering, Design, Marketing, Sales, HR/People & Culture, Product Management, Customer Support/Success, Operations, Finance & Accounting, IT/Infrastructure, Legal & Compliance, and Data & Analytics.

## Stats at a glance

The header shows Total Staff (with an active count), Average Salary, Average Rating, and number of Departments in use.

## Who it's for

Anyone maintaining a company roster or looking up an employee's role, department, or contact details.
