---
title: HR Records User Guide
summary: Step-by-step instructions for managing employee records.
---

## Adding an employee

1. Click **Add Employee** (or **Add First Employee** if your list is empty).
2. Fill in the **Personal info** section: First name, Last name, Email, and Phone.
3. Fill in **Role & department**: choose a Department, enter a Job title, and pick an Employment type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Intern).
4. Fill in **Administrative** details: Start date, Base salary, and Reporting manager.
5. Click **Add employee**. First name, Last name, Email, Department, Job title, and Start date are all required — the button won't do anything until they're filled in.

## Viewing a profile

Click a card, row, or use the "⋮" menu → **View Profile** to see status, employment type, department, salary, contact info (email, phone, employee ID, manager, location, and join date).

## Messaging an employee

Click **Message** on a card or in the profile view, set a Priority, write a Subject and body, and send. This records a confirmation on screen but does not deliver an actual message — use it as a personal reminder rather than a real notification to the employee.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search box (name, email, or ID) and the department pills to narrow the list. Switch between Grid and List view with the toggle.

## Exporting

Click the header **Export** icon to download your current employee list as a CSV, including salary and performance figures.

## Removing an employee

Use the "⋮" menu, list row, or profile view's delete icon. You'll be asked to type **DELETE** to confirm — this is a safeguard against accidental removal, since the action is permanent.
