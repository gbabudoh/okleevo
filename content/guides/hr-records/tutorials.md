title: HR Records Tutorials
summary: Full workflows for common HR record-keeping scenarios.
---

## Tutorial: Onboarding a new hire

1. Click **Add Employee** and fill in their personal details, department, job title, and employment type.
2. Set their start date and base salary, and note their reporting manager.
3. Once saved, they immediately appear under their department's filter pill with a live count update.

## Tutorial: Finding everyone in a department

1. Click the department pill you're interested in (e.g. Engineering) to filter the list.
2. Switch to List view if you want a denser, table-style read of the whole department at once.

## Tutorial: Looking up a specific person quickly

1. Type their name, email, or employee ID into the search box.
2. Click their card or row to open the full profile, including tenure and contact details.

## Tutorial: Exporting your roster for payroll or reporting

1. Optionally filter to the department or subset you need.
2. Click the header **Export** icon to download a CSV with everyone's role, department, salary, and other details.

## Tutorial: Offboarding an employee

1. Find their record and open the "⋮" menu (or their profile) and choose to remove them.
2. Type **DELETE** in the confirmation field to complete the removal — double-check you have the right person first, since this can't be undone.
