Inventory is a real-time stock tracking module — add products, watch quantity against reorder thresholds, log every stock movement, print barcode labels, and run inventory reports.

## What it's for

- Recording every product with SKU, category, quantity, unit price, supplier, and location
- Automatically flagging items as **In Stock**, **Low Stock**, **Out of Stock**, or **Overstocked**
- Logging stock movements — stock in, stock out, or an absolute stocktake adjustment
- Printing barcode labels and running valuation/category reports
- Getting a low-stock notification the moment an item crosses its reorder point

## Stats at a glance

The header shows Stock Value (£), Total Units, Alerts (low + out of stock combined), and total Products.

## Who it's for

Anyone responsible for keeping physical or digital stock levels accurate and avoiding running out of (or over-ordering) product.

## Good to know

Stock value and currency are shown in £ throughout this module.
